# Trademarks

The code of tripbox is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 (see `LICENSE`
and `NOTICE`). The licence covers the code. It gives no right to the name "tripbox" or to its logo (the icons in
`src/tripbox/web/static/`), which stay reserved to the project.

## What you may do without asking

- Run, copy and share unmodified copies of the program under its own name.
- Say what your work is built on: "based on tripbox", "a fork of tripbox", "works with tripbox".
- Name the project in articles, talks, reviews, tutorials and comparisons.
- Keep the attribution line that `NOTICE` requires at the foot of Settings, the sign-in page and a shared trip's
  page.

## What needs a new name

A version whose code you have changed is your own, so it needs a name and a logo of its own:

- Rename it: its page titles, the sign-in page's heading, the web manifest, the calendar feed's name, the
  notifications and the images' descriptions say your name, not "tripbox".
- Replace the logo with one of your own that cannot be mistaken for it.
- Do not suggest that the project made, checked or supports your version.

Keeping the line "Based on tripbox, © 2026 …" from `NOTICE` in the Appropriate Legal Notices your version's
interfaces display (in this program the foot of Settings, the sign-in page and a shared trip's page) is how a
renamed version credits the original; it does not make the version "tripbox".
